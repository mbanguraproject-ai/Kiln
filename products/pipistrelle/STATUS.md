- Step 5 (the Roost, the owner's note of 14 October): the four ways to play (Nightly Cave, Long Night, Hangar, Journal) are compact tiles in the belfry's two top corners, each with a chip for the number that matters and a line for what you will find (tonight's biome and the real moon's phase, your deepest cave, the bat you fly, scratches read); only the Long Rain and Requests sit at the bottom. The Long Rain shows the next cave you will fly: a picture of it painted in its biome's colours (its stone, water, crystal, owls or moss, the pups waiting, your bat, the echo route to the moonlit arch), its mission, its Moons, a progress bar per biome, and Fly starts it at once. The belfry is alive: clouds cross the moon so the light comes and goes, the moonbeam sways, dust turns in it, moths circle the light, wild bats cross the moon, a lantern flickers, pups sway and call to each other, your bats stretch and fly laps of the belfry snapping up moths. Tap to chirp (the echo ring lights what it reaches and pups answer), tap a pup (its group calls back), tap your bat (it flies a lap), tap the window (the clouds part). Nothing hangs behind a tile or a card, on any screen shape (tests: four sizes, the taps, the Long Rain's Fly).
- Step 6 (the Roost polished, the owner's note of 14 October): the four tiles are 13% shorter and 12% narrower (122 x 85 px on a phone, was 138 x 98) and always the same size, titles whole even on a 360 px phone. The belfry hangs only as many beams as the colony needs; the old colony sleeps in the dark at the beams' free ends (eyes shut until a sound reaches them), and below the beams the lower belfry is alive: the colony circles as moonlit silhouettes, mist lies on the floor where the moonbeam lands, fireflies rise, and a bell rope hangs from the lowest beam (pull it and the bell tolls through the belfry). The Long Rain card: the title leads, a quiet "Next cave" tag (Fly is the only gold thing on it), the picture labels the route from You to the Moonlit arch with arrows along it, and one progress row with matching parts (this cave's Moons, a bar per biome, the Moons count). Requests: one segment per request (part-filled as you get closer), the count right over the bar's end.
