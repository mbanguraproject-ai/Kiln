Drag a piece from the tray onto the grid. Fill a row or column to clear it; clear again within three moves to keep a combo going. Every tray can be placed in some order, and the game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Each line you clear becomes a wedge of your rose window, the medallion by the score. Rings complete at 16, 48 and 112 lines. Combo ×3 lines get a gold edge, and a line of one glaze turns to gold leaf. When the run ends, the window is fired and hung on your studio wall.

Keyboard: press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a piece, move it with the arrow keys, then press Enter.
