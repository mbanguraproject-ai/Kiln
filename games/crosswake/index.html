Tap the pond to place two stones. When you press Drop, each sends out a ripple. Where the two ripples cross, a spark runs across the water and lights every lantern it touches.

Dropped together, the spark runs in a straight line halfway between the stones. Make the second stone wait and the line bends around it. A lit lantern ripples too: its sparks fly straight out, away from each stone.

Every drop costs two stones. Light every lantern to clear the pond. Unused stones are worth 50 each.
